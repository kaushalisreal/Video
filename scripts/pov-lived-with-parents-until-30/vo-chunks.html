POV: You Lived With Your Parents Until 30 — Then Bought a House in Cash. Voiceover boxes

The script (about 15.5 minutes at your pace) is split into 22 boxes of 75 to 100 words, ready for Google Vids. The dialogue stays inside the narration in quotes, so every box uses the same narrator voice. Paste one box, generate it, then tick it off. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

WorkflowSet the narrator voice once in Vids, then go top to bottom: copy a box, paste, generate. Name each export with the file name shown, and the clips will sort into the right order for the edit.
TagsEvery tag comes from the Vids menus. If a tag gets read out loud instead of performed, delete it and re-add it with Apply audio tags.
