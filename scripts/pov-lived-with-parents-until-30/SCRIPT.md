## The money math (keep consistent everywhere)

All returns assume **7% a year after inflation** (a long-run stock market average, not a promise).

| Age | Monthly saved | Balance at end of age |
|---|---|---|
| 22–24 | $1,800 | $46K at 24 |
| 25–27 | $2,300 | $105K at 26, $141K at 27 (after the dip and recovery) |
| 28–29 | $2,800 | $180K at 28, **$279K at 30** |

- **Salary:** $52K at 22, about $3,400/month take-home. Pays parents **$400/month**.
- **Rent avoided:** $1,400/month (plus about $200 for utilities and furniture).
- **Total put in:** about $215K. **Grew to:** about $279K.
- **Tax on the gains:** about $10K (15% long-term capital gains on about $64K).
- **House:** **$255,000**, 3 bedrooms, in a smaller town 40 minutes away. Plus a few thousand in closing costs.
- **Emergency fund:** $15K kept separately in savings, never touched.
- **After buying:** property tax plus insurance of about $350/month. Invests **$2,500/month** → about **$800K at 45**.
- **Maya's rent over 8 years:** $1,400 → $1,750, about **$148K** in total.
- **Ryan's lease:** $550/month for about 5 years, **$30K+** for a car he never owned.
- **Market dip at 27:** −$18K in three weeks, about 8 months of savings. Recovered within a year.
- **Parents' mortgage payoff at 32:** $41K.

⚠️ Before publishing, update rent and house prices to current averages if you want them exact. The story works as long as the numbers stay consistent with each other.

## Voice and delivery guide

- **Narrator:** calm, warm, measured male or neutral voice. Speak slowly, never salesy.
- **Pace:** 120–130 wpm. **Pause for 1–1.5 seconds** after key lines: "It's cash." / "Then write it down." / "Then stop looking at it." / "I guess that room paid rent after all."
- **Numbers:** say them slowly and clearly. They are the proof.
- **Dialogue lines:** a slightly softer, conversational voice (or a second voice actor for Mom and Dad).
- **Music:** soft piano and ambient pads. Drop the music out completely for the 2 AM kitchen scene and the receipt scene, then bring it back gently.
