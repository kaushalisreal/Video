POV: You Got Promoted 5 Times and Still Can't Quit. Voiceover chunks

The script (about 20 minutes at your pace, 3,139 words) is split into short pieces of 1 to 4 sentences, ready for Google Vids. Paste one chunk per scene, generate it, then tick it off. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

WorkflowFilter by one voice, set that voice once in Vids, and generate all of its chunks back to back. Then switch voice. Name each export with the file name shown, and the clips will sort into the right order for the edit.
TagsEvery tag comes from the Vids menus. If a tag gets read out loud instead of performed, delete it and re-add it with Apply audio tags.
