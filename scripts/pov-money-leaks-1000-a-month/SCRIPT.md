## The money math (keep consistent)

**Household:** you + Tess ≈ **$110K/year**.

| Leak | $/month |
|---|---|
| Forgotten or unused subscriptions (of $247 total; ~$79 kept) | 168 |
| Convenience tax (delivery markups, fees, tips) | 210 |
| Bank fees (overdraft + $12 maintenance + ATM) | 55 |
| Credit card interest ($6,700 × 22% ÷ 12) | 123 |
| Loyalty tax (phone $90 + car insurance $45) | 135 |
| Lazy cash ($15,000 × ~4% ÷ 12) | 50 |
| Impulse buys + buy-now-pay-later late fees (~$210) + annual fees and add-ons (~$62) | 272 |
| **Total** | **≈ $1,013** (≈ $12,156/year) |

- **The 17 subscriptions:** 4 streaming ($72), 2 music, 2 cloud storage, gym, fitness app, language app, news site, magazine, meditation app, phone warranty, delivery membership, and one mystery charge.
- **Card payoff:** $150/month → 95 months (~8 yrs), $7,408 interest. $950/month ($800 + $150) → **8 months**.
- **Investing:** $800/month at 7% for 30 years ≈ **$975,977** ("close to a million"). The full $1,000/month would be ≈ $1.22M.
- **Lazy cash:** $15,000 × (4.00% − 0.01%) ≈ $599/year ≈ **$50/month**.
