## The money math (keep consistent everywhere)

**Household:** you $150K + Nina $50K = **$200K**. Married filing jointly, 2 kids, 2026 rules.

| Item | Yearly | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 401(k) (6% of $200K) | $12,000 | pre-tax, to get the match |
| Health insurance (family) | $6,000 | pre-tax |
| Social Security + Medicare | ~$15,000 | 7.65% on ~$194K of wages |
| Federal income tax | ~$18,000 | taxable ≈ $182K − $32,200 standard deduction ≈ $150K → ~$22.4K − $4,400 child tax credit ($2,200 × 2) |
| State income tax | ~$8,000 | assumes ~4.5% |
| **Take-home** | **~$141,000 ≈ $11,800/month** | |

| Monthly budget (before the reset) | $ |
|---|---|
| House (P&I $3,792 + tax ~$750 + insurance ~$250) | 4,800 |
| Two cars (payments $1,370 + insurance + gas) | 1,700 |
| Daycare (Max) | 1,600 |
| Groceries | 1,100 |
| Takeout | 600 |
| Utilities + phones + internet | 500 |
| Student loans | 450 |
| Kids' activities | 350 |
| Subscriptions | 250 |
| Shopping | 400 |
| Vacations | 400 |
| Home repairs + everything else | 350 |
| **Total** | **12,500** → **−$700/month** |

- **Mortgage check:** $600,000 at 6.5% for 30 years = $3,792/month.
- **Credit card:** $23,000 at ~22% APR ≈ $420/month in interest.
- **Reset savings:** car −$460, subscriptions −$160, takeout −$350, shopping −$200+, vacations −$200 ≈ **$1,400/month** → about +$700/month. Kindergarten turns $1,600 of daycare into $400 of after-school care, freeing **$1,200/month**.
- **Investing:** $2,000/month at 7% from 40 to 65 ≈ **$1.6M**, so "more than a million" is safe.
- **Millionaire Next Door rule:** 38 × $200,000 ÷ 10 = **$760,000** expected net worth. Your family: under $380K (less than half, what the book calls an "under accumulator of wealth"). Luis: $600K+ on at most $85K a year.
