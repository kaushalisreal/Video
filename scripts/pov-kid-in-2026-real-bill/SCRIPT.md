## The money math (keep consistent)

**Household:** you ~$80K + Maya $70K = **~$150K** before taxes. Maya's take-home ≈ $4,400/month.

| First-year line | $ |
|---|---|
| Birth, out of pocket (high-deductible plan) | 3,200 |
| Family health insurance increase ($400 × 12) | 4,800 |
| Maya's lost income: 6 weeks at 60% + 6 weeks unpaid on $70K ($1,346/week) | 11,300 |
| Gear (mostly used/gifted; car seat new) | 2,500 |
| Diapers and wipes (~$80/month) | 960 |
| Formula and food | 1,500 |
| Daycare from month 4 ($1,700 × 9) | 15,300 |
| Bigger apartment (+$600 × 12) | 7,200 |
| Copays, clothes, everything else | 1,500 |
| **Total** | **≈ $48,260** |

- **Average year implied by $300K:** $300K ÷ 18 ≈ $16.7K, so year one is almost 3×.
- **Tax help:** child tax credit $2,200 + dependent care FSA $7,500 × ~33% (22% federal + 7.65% FICA + ~4% state) ≈ $2,500 → **≈ $4,700** ("about five thousand"). Plus the $1,000 Trump Account seed in the child's name.
- **Practice run:** daycare $1,700 + rent $600 + insurance $400 + extras $300 = **$3,000/month** × 6 = **$18,000** (covers the $11.3K of lost leave income + the $3.2K birth + most of the gear).
- **529:** $200/month for 18 years at 6% ≈ **$77,470** (contributions $43,200).
- **College today:** in-state public tuition and fees $11,950 + room and board $13,900 ≈ **$25,850/year** (2025–26).
