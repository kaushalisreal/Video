## 2. Patterns

1. **Show the impossible in one picture: water higher than the houses.** All three big Netherlands breakouts from small channels (232x, 44x, 20x) use the same layout. On one side is a wall/dike with the sea at the top edge, often with a wave. On the other side, a town with red roofs sits clearly *lower*. A viewer understands "this country is below the sea" in under a second, without reading anything.
2. **Above-water vs below-water cutaways win** (Venice 256x, "Too Late?" 30x). Splitting the frame at the waterline into "surface" and "what's under" creates a curiosity gap.
3. **A single number with a scale marker:** "7m" with a measuring arrow, "2 Meters Below Sea Level", "$12 BILLION". The number makes the danger feel real.
4. **Very little text: 2–5 words, often a question that sounds sceptical** ("HOW IS THIS EVEN SAFE?", "WHAT'S UNDER VENICE?", "TOO LATE?"). Thumbnails that repeat the full title as text (Real Engineering, History Scope, Johnny Harris) did average or worse.
5. **Look: bright, saturated, very realistic 3D.** Turquoise/deep-blue sea, orange-red Dutch roofs, green fields, clear daylight, high camera angle. Flat cartoons and plain maps underperform unless the channel is already big.
6. **Threat implied, disaster not shown.** The wave is *about to* hit, and the town is still dry. Tension works better than a picture of the disaster happening.
7. **No faces needed.** None of the outliers here use a presenter's face, which suits a faceless narration channel.

## 3. Angles from *this* script that competitors haven't used

- Sea higher than the houses (proven layout; safest bet). Script: "a quarter of the country lies below sea level"
- **They flooded their own country on purpose** (1574 Leiden / 1672 Water Line). Script hook at 0:28. No competitor thumbnail uses this.
- **A city on the bottom of a sea** (Almere / Flevoland). Script: "built a city on the bottom of a sea"
- **8,000 new homes at the lowest point**, 22 ft (6.7 m) below the sea (2026 plan). Timely, and gives an argument to have in the comments.

## 5. Text tips (when you add it)
- 2–5 words, all caps, heavy sans-serif (Montserrat Black / Anton), white with a dark stroke or a yellow highlight on one word.
- Don't repeat the video title. Write the sceptical question the picture raises.
- Add one number with a measuring arrow if possible (7 m, 22 ft, −6.7 m).
- Test at 160x90 px. If the sea-above-houses idea isn't obvious at that size, push the height difference further.
