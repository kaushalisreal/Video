# What Do Billionaires Actually Do All Day?

- **Version:** 2, rewritten in the style of Ink Explainer's "What Did Ancient Humans Actually Do All Day?" (7.7M views)
- **Runtime target:** under 12 min
- **Narration:** 1,403 words, about 11.1 min at 126.5 wpm (your measured pace)
- **Tone:** curious mini-documentary, calm and confident. Very few jokes. Every fact ties back to the viewer's own life.
- **Visual style:** hand-drawn 2D stick-figure cartoon
- **Narration only:** `narration.txt`. The first comedy version is kept in `narration-v1-comedy.txt`.

## Characters (paste the full description into every image prompt)

**YOU (the viewer):** stick figure, large round white head, simple minimal face, messy brown hair, thin black stick limbs, thick black outlines, wrinkled blue work shirt with a loose tie, always holding a glowing phone.

**THE BILLIONAIRE (main subject):** stick figure, large round white head, calm simple face, short neat grey hair, navy crew-neck sweater, beige chinos, relaxed posture, usually holding a coffee mug or a newspaper.

**THE READER (Buffett-inspired):** elderly stick figure, white hair, thick black glasses, grey suit, surrounded by stacks of paper. Inspired by, not a portrait of.

**THE SCHEDULER (Gates-inspired):** stick figure with a light brown side-part, round glasses, pale blue sweater over a collared shirt. Inspired by, not a portrait of.

**THE RESEARCHERS:** two stick figures in lab coats with clipboards and stopwatches.
